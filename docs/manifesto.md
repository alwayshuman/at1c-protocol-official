# AT1C Protocol — Manifesto

**AT1C Protocol believes identity belongs to the individual, not the platform.**

A person should own their sovereign identity as surely as they own a deed to their life: portable, inheritable, revocable only by the rightful owner, and usable across systems without asking a monopoly for permission. Users should not be corralled into fenced ecosystems, trapped by hidden terms, or reduced to profiles inside someone else's machine.

**We believe AI agents should serve humanity, not control it.**

An agent may act only under explicit, verifiable authority granted by the person it serves. That authority must be transparent, scoped, auditable, and easy to withdraw. Consent should be a living right, not a checkbox buried in a platform's maze.

**We reject the model where a few corporations become the gatekeepers of human existence online.**

No company should own the keys to your digital life, your contacts, your work, your memory, or your voice. No platform should be able to turn freedom into dependency, or participation into surveillance.

**We call for a free internet built on open standards, open source, user-owned credentials, and interoperable permissions.**

Humanity can build systems where people and intelligent agents collaborate as equals in purpose, not subjects in a cage. The future should be transparent, decentralised, and accountable to the people who live in it.

Ownership of self is the foundation. Freedom is the AT1C Protocol. Humans first.
