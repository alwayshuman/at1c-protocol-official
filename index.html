Legal foundation

AT1C is the evidentiary infrastructure AI accountability law requires

Legal scholars have identified the core gap: when an AI agent causes harm, liability attaches to the human who approved the action — but only if there is evidence of what they approved. Without a verifiable consent record, accountability collapses.

AT1C's signed receipt is exactly that evidence. Every receipt is a timestamped, cryptographically signed record that a specific human explicitly approved a specific agent action. It transforms the legal question from "did the principal exercise reasonable care?" — hard to prove, subjective — to "is there a signed receipt?" — verifiable, objective, on the record.
