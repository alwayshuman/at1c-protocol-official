Why AT1C

Non-custodial

AT1C never holds signing keys. Agent keys and user keys stay on their respective devices.

Verifiable proof

Every receipt is cryptographically signed. Any party can verify independently — no trust required.

Replay protection

Nonce-based single-use receipts. An approval for one action can never be reused for another.

Open source

MIT licensed. Inspect the protocol, fork it, wrap your existing system. No lock-in.

EU AI Act compliance

The EU AI Act requires accountability for automated decisions, human oversight of high-risk AI actions, and auditable records of AI behaviour. AT1C gives you all three out of the box. Enforcement begins August 2026.
